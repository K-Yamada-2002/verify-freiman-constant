# Earlier `131` research

The unfinished `131`-forbidden problem, its generalized T-interval searches,
alternative kernels, and small obstruction checks are retained here.
These are separate from the adopted `31313` and `31313,13131` tables.

`check_all.py` runs the earlier small exact checks and collects historical
search results. `prepare_t.py`, `t_kernel.cpp`, `anchor_kernel.cpp`,
`generalized_t.py`, and `search_t.py` belong to that earlier research route.
Obstruction checks can prove a particular gap without settling whether the
whole Cantor sum has interior.

The [project README](../README.md) retains the detailed scope of those results.
Old commands are preserved through `src/` links. For the current table and
interval certificates, start with [verify/](../verify/README.md) and
[search/](../search/README.md).
